Measure in

Counts of people from Wikidata: country populations, language speakers, Instagram followers, yearly visitors and passengers, venue capacity, and a few others. Each count under a dropdown links to its Wikidata entry. Wikidata is edited by volunteers, so dates and methods vary from count to count. Left out: visitor and passenger counts under 1,000 a year (wrong in Wikidata), entries with no name, and vaccination and view counts (they count doses and views, not people). Data: people_counts.json.
