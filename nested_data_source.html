Nested data source

All areas are in square kilometers. Square miles are converted from km² (1 sq mi = 2.589988 km²). States, metro areas and countries are land area, leaving out water. Lakes and reservoirs are water surface area. US states and metro areas use land area from the Census Bureau's 2024 boundary files, which give square meters. Countries use land area from the World Bank (FAO) for 2024, which leaves out inland water such as major rivers and lakes. Check: Census land for the 50 states plus DC sums to 9,151,638 km², and the World Bank gives 9,147,420 km² for the US. Metro areas are the 393 metropolitan statistical areas, sorted by 2024 population. Lakes and reservoirs are every one of 5,000 km² or more in HydroLAKES, a global dataset that calculates each lake's area from its outline the same way for every lake. It is a peer-reviewed research dataset, not official figures. Data: nested_data_source.csv. Shapes: areas/.
