1 atom = 1 step

There are a million atoms across 1 strand of hair.

If we split a hair in half, it is 500,000 atoms.

And if we split it again, it is 250,000 atoms.

An atom is very very small, but if you can imagine... if 1 step you take is 1 atom

Then a hair is 1,000,000 steps.

You are inches tall, each of your steps is about 20 inches, so to walk across a hair would be like walking 316 miles.
